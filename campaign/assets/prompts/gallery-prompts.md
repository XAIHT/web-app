# XAIHT / Tlamatini Campaign Gallery Prompts

## Production Notes

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation (`image_gen`), raster PNG output.
- Mode: new campaign scenes derived from the master visual reference.
- Master reference: `../gallery/00-tlamatini-master-reference.png`.
- Intended use: website launch page, campaign collateral, investor deck, strategic deck, and impact paper.
- Output ratio: wide 16:9 unless a derivative crop is explicitly required later.

## Shared Direction

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a wide 16:9 campaign gallery image using the supplied reference as the exact recurring identity for Tlamatini: the same late-thirties adult Latina/mestiza technical operator, same face, dark shoulder-length hair, practical charcoal workwear, subtle earpiece, restrained maize-gold and cyan stitching. Premium photorealistic editorial technology photography, physically plausible equipment, soft directional light, Obsidian, Chalk, Maize, Mineral Jade, Electric Cyan, and restrained Oxide Coral accents. Show one clear task, a human decision, Tlamatini as an active collaborator, and a credible result. No readable marketing copy, no company logos, no watermark, no fantasy holograms across faces, no sexualization, no glossy bodysuit, no armor, no superhero pose, no neon cyberpunk, no glowing eyes, no plastic skin, no impossible machinery, no passive beneficiary.
