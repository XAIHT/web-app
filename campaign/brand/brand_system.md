# XAIHT and Tlamatini Brand System

## Brand idea

XAIHT is the company that makes powerful artificial intelligence answerable to human intent.

Tlamatini is the product expression of that idea: knowledgeable, calm, technically exact, and present where software meets machines.

The identity must feel advanced without looking magical, military, or dystopian. It should communicate command, care, legibility, and real work.

## Brand hierarchy

- **XAIHT:** company and trust mark.
- **Tlamatini:** flagship AI operator and primary product name.
- **XAIHT / Tlamatini:** paired signature for launch materials.
- **Command the Whole Workspace:** campaign platform.

## Name expansion

**XAIHT:** Extended Artificial Intelligence, Humanly Tempered.

Use the expansion in investor, press, and long-form materials. Use the short wordmark in product and campaign UI.

## Taglines

Primary company line: **Intelligence under human command.**

Primary product line: **Your machine. Your agents. Your command.**

Campaign line: **Command the whole workspace.**

Supporting line: **One operator across code, engines, hardware, and workflows.**

## Logo concept

The XAIHT mark is a compact geometric `X` built from two crossing paths around a protected central aperture. The paths represent human intent and machine execution. The open center represents review, evidence, and the fact that no action is hidden from the operator.

The Tlamatini product mark uses the same aperture inside a stepped knowledge frame. It may suggest a page, circuit, and horizon without copying a historic or sacred glyph.

Requirements for the final logo suite:

- Vector master in SVG.
- Horizontal and compact signatures.
- XAIHT-only company mark.
- XAIHT / Tlamatini paired lockup.
- One-color white, one-color black, and full-color variants.
- Clear-space rule equal to the central aperture width.
- Minimum digital width: 24 px for the mark, 96 px for the wordmark.
- No glow, bevel, gradient, or hairline detail in the master mark.

## Color system

### Core

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Obsidian | `#090B0C` | Primary field and dark wordmark |
| Chalk | `#F2F3EE` | Primary text and light field |
| Maize | `#D6A84B` | Human intent, key actions, company mark |
| Mineral Jade | `#42A783` | Verified state, local operation, product accents |

### Signal accents

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electric Cyan | `#36B7D9` | Data, connectivity, creative engines |
| Oxide Coral | `#E56855` | Warnings, attention, physical action |
| Iris | `#8B78D3` | Vision, voice, multimodal systems |

### Rules

- Obsidian and Chalk carry the interface; accents identify meaning.
- Maize is not a generic luxury gold. Use it for human decision points and the company signature.
- Jade indicates verified or locally controlled states.
- Coral is reserved for consequential action, risk, and cancellation.
- Cyan and Iris keep the system from collapsing into a one-note black-and-gold palette.
- Do not use full-screen gradients or decorative glowing orbs.

## Typography

- Display: **Space Grotesk** or **IBM Plex Sans**, 600-700 weight.
- Body: **Inter**, 400-600 weight.
- Technical labels: **IBM Plex Mono**, 400-600 weight.
- Use normal letter spacing for sentences and headings.
- Uppercase is limited to short labels, release identifiers, and the XAIHT wordmark.
- Large display text belongs only in true hero moments; compact panels use compact type.

## Tlamatini portrayal

Tlamatini is not a mascot, pin-up, hologram goddess, or anonymous robot. She is a senior technical operator represented with calm human presence and subtle machine cues.

### Stable visual traits

- Adult Latina or mestiza appearance without using a real person's identity.
- Dark hair, practical and consistent across scenes.
- Functional charcoal workwear with narrow Maize, Jade, Cyan, or Iris signal details.
- No exposed armor, sexualized bodysuit, fantasy weapon, crown, or superhero pose.
- Eye contact only when the composition calls for direct address; most scenes show her working beside a human operator.
- Human operators remain visible in consequential scenarios.

### Product interface representation

- Show real code, workflow nodes, terminal evidence, device traces, scene views, or review controls.
- Avoid unreadable walls of fake neon glyphs.
- Keep screens secondary to the person and task, not pasted over faces.
- Do not place tiny fake text in generated images when website or deck typography can carry the claim cleanly.

## Photography and campaign imagery

Visual direction: editorial technology photography with documentary credibility and selective near-future detail.

### Composition

- Real environments: workshop, studio, lab, small factory, classroom, field station, operations room, home office.
- One clear task per image.
- Natural working posture and believable equipment.
- A visible relationship between human decision, Tlamatini guidance, and physical or digital result.
- Reserve clean negative space for campaign copy, but keep the product or use case inspectable.

### Lighting

- Neutral daylight, practical task lighting, or restrained cinematic contrast.
- Maize/Jade/Cyan accents may appear in interfaces or edge lighting.
- Avoid dark atmospheric images where the actual product and task disappear.

### Regional representation

- Represent different parts of the world through credible workplaces, tools, climate, materials, and people, not costume shorthand.
- Do not exoticize rural or low-income contexts.
- Show agency: local professionals are operators and owners, not passive beneficiaries.
- Include women and people of different ages in technical authority roles.

## Icon and layout language

- Use Lucide icons in the website where an existing symbol fits.
- Use square or gently rounded geometry; card radius stays at 8 px or below.
- Use lines, tracks, apertures, and stepped frames as recurring motifs.
- Avoid nested cards, decorative blobs, and generic dashboard grids.
- Diagrams should show real sequence or ownership and remain sparse.

## Voice

### Personality

- Calm, capable, direct.
- Ambitious about the work, modest about proof.
- Technically literate without jargon theater.
- Human-centered without sentimental language.

### Preferred verbs

Know, inspect, ask, approve, build, connect, run, observe, cancel, reuse, verify.

### Avoid

Revolutionary, omniscient, limitless, fully autonomous, infallible, replaces your team, guaranteed secure.
